## 1. Planning Determination

As codified in `PHIL_Evolution_Earth.md`, the *Evolution Earth* universe is built upon five foundational philosophical commitments:
1. **Knowledge is earned, not given:** Understanding requires effort, encounter, and consequence; truth is never handed down as unearned revelation.
2. **Humanity is not fixed:** Transformation is biological, moral, social, and technological; the boundary of what remains "human" is the central contested terrain.
3. **Competing identities precede competing moralities:** Deepest conflicts do not stem from simplistic "Good vs. Evil", but from rival conceptions of *what deserves to continue*.
4. **Responsibility persists under pressure:** Characters possess agency; guilt is evidence of agency, and redemption is possible because they could have chosen otherwise.
5. **Progress is transformative, not directional:** Change carries heavy cost regardless of advancement or regression, and the price is paid by individuals, not abstract history.

The universal governing philosophical question is:
> **"What deserves to survive?"**

Generic drafting engines often degrade high-concept philosophical storytelling into conventional melodrama (e.g. evil cartoon antagonists, free epiphanies, pain-free technological shortcuts). RM-036 creates an analytical diagnostic that stress-tests scenes and character arcs against Tony Latimer's philosophical foundation.

---

## 2. Objective

Provide an advisory diagnostic that evaluates scenes and arcs for philosophical fidelity:
1. **Unearned Knowledge Detector:** Flags moments where characters arrive at key truths without paying an empirical, tactical, or emotional price.
2. **Moral Melodrama Auditor:** Ensures antagonistic factions (Church of KAO, Fourth Faction, corporate conglomerates, CIETTA graduates) operate from coherent, competing survival doctrines rather than generic villainy.
3. **Individual Cost Accounting:** Audits technological advancements (neural restructuring, *The Sight*, energy-state integration) to ensure biological, psychological, or social tolls are authentically felt by characters.
4. **Governing Question Alignment:** Tests whether the dramatic core of a scene meaningfully addresses *"What deserves to survive?"*

---

## 3. Initial Scope

- **Commitment Audit Heuristics:** Advisory pattern matching evaluating scene conflict dynamics against the 5 commitments.
- **Cost/Consequence Matrix:** Tracks character sacrifices, biological fatigue, and psychological residue across chapters.
- **Faction Philosophy Profiles:** Evaluates faction behaviors against their internal survival logic.
- **Reporting:** Narrative diagnostic integrated alongside RM-021 critique personas, providing targeted questions and observations for the author's consideration.
