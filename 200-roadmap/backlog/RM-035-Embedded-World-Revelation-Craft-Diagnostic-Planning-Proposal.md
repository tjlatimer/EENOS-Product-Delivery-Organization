## 1. Planning Determination

Standard editorial and writing tools evaluate prose using generic conventions (e.g. "show vs. tell," passive voice, readability indexes). None evaluate the universe-native craft laws developed specifically for *Evolution Earth* in `Embedded World Revelation (EWR).md`.

The core law of Evolution Earth worldbuilding is:
> **"If the world doesn't interfere with what a character wants, it doesn't get explained."**

To maintain immersive tension without informational bloat, all world rules, technologies (BuilderBots, DCOP, decanting, energy states), factions, and history must be revealed through Tony Latimer's five native EWR techniques. RM-035 establishes a dedicated diagnostic to audit drafted scenes against these five techniques.

---

## 2. Objective

Provide a specialized craft diagnostic engine that evaluates human-authored prose against the 5 EWR techniques:
1. **Contextual Friction (`[EWR-FRICTION]`):** Verify that world mechanics appear only when creating obstacles to character intent.
2. **Loaded Dialogue (`[EWR-DIALOGUE]`):** Ensure dialogue raises questions rather than answering them, with characters speaking as cultural natives who take the world for granted.
3. **POV Filtering (`[EWR-POV-FILTER]`):** Verify that each viewpoint character perceives the environment through their specific psychological and observational filter:
   - *Noah:* Systems, network patterns, anomalies, mechanical connections.
   - *Gabriel:* Perception control, narrative manipulation, institutional leverage.
   - *Sumitra:* Somatic/physical tells, emotional currents, hidden body language.
   - *Belosi:* Power dynamics, vulnerability, commodification, transaction value.
4. **Historical Residue (`[EWR-RESIDUE]`):** Confirm the past is revealed through physical scars, broken systems, and architectural residue rather than exposition blocks or flashbacks.
5. **Asymmetry Ladder (`[EWR-ASYMMETRY]`):** Ensure the reader earns understanding by observing characters collide with systems.

---

## 3. Initial Scope

- **Exposition Block Detection:** Scans for standalone explanatory paragraphs lacking active character action, immediate conflict, or sensory friction.
- **POV Filter Verification:** Evaluates character observation vocabulary against the active character's perceptual bias profile.
- **Dialogue Economy & Weight:** Detects "helpful to the reader" dialogue lines (as-you-know-Bob exposition) and recommends loaded conversational alternatives.
- **Reporting:** Structured, non-destructive advisory diagnostic giving line-level feedback under the RM-021 critique persona framework.

---

## 4. Exclusions & Guardrails

- No automatic rewriting of prose or altering scene text.
- Strictly zero numerical scoring, letter grades, or formulaic percentage gates.
- Advisory-only observations designed to support the author's editorial revision pass (RM-020).
