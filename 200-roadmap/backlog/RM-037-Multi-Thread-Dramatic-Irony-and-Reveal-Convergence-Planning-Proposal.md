## 1. Planning Determination

*Evolution Earth* weaves multiple primary narrative threads across distinct factions, geographies, and viewpoints:
- **Gabriel Lightner:** The investigative, guilt-laden search for Sumitra, confronting the legacy of Athena's departures and neural experiments.
- **Noah Covarrin Lightner:** The coming-of-age awakening, internal partnership with Irma, and conscious bridge to *The We*.
- **Sumitra (Sumi):** Navigating the monastic hierarchy of the Church of KAO, experiencing *The Sight*, and piecing together her somatic anomalies without knowing her origin.

The emotional impact of the series depends not merely on the quality of individual scenes, but on the **Dramatic Irony Gap**:
* The reader gains macro-knowledge by reading all three threads.
* The characters move forward in dangerous ignorance of each other's proximity and connections.
* The threads must converge toward high-stakes structural collision points without stalling or collapsing the core mysteries prematurely.

RM-037 introduces narrative modeling tools to track and orchestrate multi-thread dramatic irony and reveal convergence.

---

## 2. Objective

Provide multi-thread narrative analysis that:
1. Calculates the **Reader Awareness Gap** across chapters (what the audience knows vs. what individual POV characters know).
2. Measures **Revelation Velocity** across parallel plotlines to prevent information stagnation or premature revelation cascades.
3. Maps **Thread Convergence Trajectories** to highlight structural collision points across Book 1 and series milestones.
4. Identifies narrative pacing dead-zones or structural imbalances between viewpoint characters.

---

## 3. Initial Scope

- **Multi-POV Irony Model:** Relational graph connecting scene revelation events to reader awareness and character epistemic states (integrating with RM-034).
- **Revelation Velocity Tracker:** Measures the cadence of clue drops, secrets earned, and false assumptions shattered across chapters.
- **Convergence Trajectory Mapping:** Projects thread intersections and synchronization gates across acts.
- **Visual Data Export:** Provides structured graph and timeline data for consumption by the RM-029 Visual Story Architecture Workspace.
